# ✈️ TripGenie AI

### Your Journey. Your Budget. Your Perfect Plan.

TripGenie AI is an **AI-powered adaptive travel planning platform** that creates personalized, budget-aware travel itineraries based on the user's destination, starting point, budget, duration, travel style, interests, and travel group.

Unlike traditional travel planners that generate a fixed itinerary, TripGenie AI allows travelers to **continuously modify and personalize their journey** using AI.

> **TripGenie AI doesn't just create a travel plan — it continuously adapts the journey to the traveler's changing needs.**

---

## 🌍 Overview

Planning a trip often requires searching across multiple platforms for:

- Places to visit
- Hotels and stay areas
- Transportation
- Food
- Activities
- Budget
- Local information
- Weather considerations
- Safety tips

TripGenie AI brings these requirements into one intelligent travel planning experience.

The platform uses **Gemma 4 31B IT** through the **Google Gemini API** to generate structured, personalized travel plans.

---

# 🚀 Key Features

## 🤖 1. AI-Powered Trip Planning

Generate a complete personalized itinerary using AI.

Users can provide:

- Starting Point
- Destination
- Travel dates
- Duration
- Number of travelers
- Budget
- Currency
- Travel style
- Interests
- Food preferences
- Accommodation preference
- Transportation preference
- Special requirements

TripGenie AI generates a structured day-by-day travel plan.

---

## 🧠 2. Adaptive AI Itinerary

TripGenie AI does not treat an itinerary as fixed.

Users can modify an existing trip using natural preferences such as:

- Make it cheaper
- Make it more relaxing
- Add adventure
- Add food experiences
- Add cultural activities
- Add photography spots
- Reduce travel time
- Make it family-friendly
- Make it couple-friendly
- Add free/low-cost activities

The AI updates the itinerary while preserving important trip constraints.

### Example

Instead of generating a completely new trip:

> "Make my Goa trip cheaper."

TripGenie AI can modify the existing itinerary and suggest lower-cost alternatives.
